Design improvements

Estimated delivery costs before checkout. Side-by-side comparison. A budget filter based on delivered cost. Undo removal. Low data mode.

Prototype scope

All interactions run in your browser. Addresses, payments, vouchers and tracking are illustrative. No account sign-in or payment processing is connected.
